About this committee

Press Corps is one of ten committees at SEAMUN I, a student-led, non-profit Model UN conference focused on global healthcare and sustainability. 100% of surplus is donated to the Thai Red Cross Society.

What is Press Corps?

Delegates step away from country blocs to operate as the frontline of information and accountability. You are allocated a news agency, cover committee sessions, interview delegates, and publish under deadline pressure.

Format

Type: Specialized · Media · Difficulty: Beginner · ROP: Special · Crisis: No. Editors lead the committee; delegates report rather than draft GA resolutions.

Conference

SEAMUN I 2027 — January 16–17 at D-PREP International School Secondary Campus (Bang Phli, Samut Prakan). Open to grades 7–12 and university students.

Fees

Delegates: 2,500 THB. Chairs/Editors: 800 THB. Photographers & advisors: 0 THB. Conference capacity: 200 delegates, up to 60 staff.

Topic

One curated topic for SEAMUN I Press Corps.

📰 The Question of Determining Journalistic Ethics in Reporting on Public Health and Global Affairs

Beginner

Delegates weigh press freedom, public interest, accuracy, and harm when covering sensitive public-health and global-affairs stories—the same ethical lines you will walk while reporting live at SEAMUN I. Research position papers and follow special media procedure once the chair report and RoP are published.
